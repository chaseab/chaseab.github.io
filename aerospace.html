Useful Vocab

Bernoulli's Principle

This princple states that as the speed of a fluid increases, pressure decreases. Using this princple, aerofoils are shaped so that the air going over the aerofoil has to travel farther around the wing thus forcing it to move at a higher speed and creating lift.
