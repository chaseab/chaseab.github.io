Useful Vocab

Bernoulli's Principle

This principle states that as the speed of a fluid increases, pressure decreases. Using this principle, aerofoils are shaped so that the air going over the aerofoil has to travel farther around the wing thus forcing it to move at a higher speed and creating lift.

TSA

Technology Student Association - a national student organization that runs engineering, design and manufacturing competitions at regional, state and national level. Flight Endurance is one of its events: build a rubber-band-powered glider and keep it in the air as long as possible.

CAD

Computer Aided Design - The use of computer-based software to aid in design processes. Typically CAM software is used by engineers to virtually design parts with 2D sketches and 3D models.

If we were tasked with this project again...

We would abandon the foam design sooner. We began with a foam design which eventually proved to be unsuccessful. Looking back, we spent too much time on this without seeing any progress. Fortunately, our team won the class contest and then maxed out all of the regional spots at our local TSA competition. This gives us the chance to go to a state and potentially national competition.
