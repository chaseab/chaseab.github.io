CSWP - Certified SolidWorks Professional

I obtained my CSWP in the fall of 2023, a globally recognized certification that validates advanced proficiency in SolidWorks software. The CSWP certification demonstrates expertise in complex part modeling, advanced assembly techniques, and detailed drawing creation. This certification requires passing rigorous exams covering advanced modeling techniques, design tables, configurations, and best practices for professional CAD work. The CSWP designation is highly valued by employers and represents the highest level of SolidWorks competency in the industry.

TSA CAD Engineering Competition

This project was completed as part of the Technology Student Association (TSA) CAD Engineering competition. The timed competition challenged participants to design and model complex mechanical assemblies using SolidWorks, demonstrating proficiency in advanced CAD techniques including parametric modeling, assembly design, and technical documentation. The project required creating detailed engineering drawings, bill of materials, and assembly instructions while meeting strict design constraints and tolerances within the time limit.
