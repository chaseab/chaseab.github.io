Where I work

SMA Actuator Research · Kennedy LabNov 2025 – present

Undergraduate researcher at VT's Hume Center, in Dr. Scott Kennedy's Intelligent Materials for Biomimetic Sensors and Actuators Lab. I build and run the test rig for parallel Flexinol wire arrays: the firing electronics, the resistance self-sensing, and the data path. Presented at the VT Hume Colloquium in May 2026; first-author paper in preparation.

MachWorksSep 2025 – present

Currently on the avionics subteam, working on servo harnessing and stress testing. Before that I ran the camera-tracking project as PM: a DSLR on a Ronin gimbal, Kalman filters fusing the aircraft's IMU and GPS, ROS 2 on a Pi.
