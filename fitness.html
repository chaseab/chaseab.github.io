01

Lifting

I've been lifting since around my sophomore year of high school, hitting a 315 squat in junior year and 225 bench in senior year. I typically run a PPL split but it can vary. I have recently been experimenting with higher volume on my SBD lifts to push nervous system adaptations for frequent and heavy loads. My current total is just over 1000, and I'm aiming to hit a 315 bench this winter and a 405 squat and deadlift this fall.

02

Swimming

I've swam basically my entire life with the ABRC Mighty Bolts. I also swam with Tide and the Ocean Lakes high school team. While I enjoy the physical aspect to swimming, I've come to learn I'm also very passionate about coaching swim (or perhaps just sports), and have done so since I began high school. From the 4–5 yo "Building Bolts" program teaching kids to swim, to the 8u–10u age group stroke work, it's been very rewarding to see the impact I can make on future generations of swimmers.

Swam: ABRC Mighty Bolts · Tide · Ocean Lakes HS
Coaching: Building Bolts (4–5 yo) → 8u–10u age groups, since freshman year

03

Running (and more…)

I've dabbled in distance running, primarily the annual Shamrock races down at the boardwalk. I ran the half marathon there in 5th grade and came back as a senior in high school to do the full marathon (quite slowly) after my swim season ended. I have mixed opinions about the sport, but I do have both a half and full Ironman on the bucket list — so I'll likely be back once I finish up my degree.

Shamrock: half marathon (5th grade) · full marathon (senior year)
Bucket list: half Ironman → full Ironman
