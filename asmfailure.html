ASM Failure Analysis Project

Eisenman Camp 2024 - Material Science Investigation

Project Overview

The Challenge

At Eisenman Camp 2024, our team was presented with a real-world engineering failure: a tire fell off a garbage truck while driving, and all the studs holding the tire broke. The tire with the other half of the studs was stolen, leaving us with critical evidence to analyze.

We were given 3 broken studs, 3 slices of the fracture points, an exemplar stud, and samples of different grades of steel to conduct a comprehensive failure analysis.
